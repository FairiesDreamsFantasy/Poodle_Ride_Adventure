# Kitchen Staff

This folder contains the characters that work in the Kitchen of Rasta-Manor.

## Characters

- **8 Cooks**: Female, White, Rastafarian. They wear vintage white dresses, indigo aprons, white stockings, and industrial white boots. Their attire is strictly "Babylon-Free" (non-sexualized).
- **14 Staff Members**: General kitchen staff assisting with various tasks.

These characters are currently implemented with generic drawing logic and will be refined with specific names and details in the future.
